restaurant-management

restaurant-management is a private, internal tool. It is used by the owner and manager of a small group of restaurants (Karmma & Kocktails, Spicy Chowk and Hidden Gem) to keep records of daily sales and business expenses.

It is not a public app and has no public users. It runs automatically on the owner's own accounts and, with the owner's permission, saves backup files, receipt photos and reports into folders and spreadsheets that the tool itself creates in the owner's Google Drive.
