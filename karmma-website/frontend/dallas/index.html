Karmma & Kocktails Dallas: Nepali & Indian Restaurant and Bar in Trinity Groves

Karmma & Kocktails is a Nepali and Indian restaurant and bar at 3011 Gulden Ln, Unit 102 in the Trinity Groves area of Dallas, Texas. Come in for momos, curries, biryani and tandoori, cocktails, or order online.

What We Serve

The Dallas menu lists every price and tags vegetarian dishes. It has 14 food sections:

  • Vegetarian Appetizers
  • Non-Vegetarian Appetizers
  • Chats
  • Soup & Salad
  • Vegetarian Classics
  • Poultry, Meat & Seafood
  • Biryani, Rice & Noodles
  • Tandoor & Grill
  • Himalayan Delights
  • Chef’s Specials
  • Momo / Dumplings
  • Naan / Breads
  • Kids Menu
  • Desserts

Cocktails and Bar

The Dallas bar serves red wines, white wines, beer & shots, martinis, cocktails & mocktails. See the Drinks tab of the Dallas menu for the full list and prices. Happy hour runs 4 PM – 7 PM.
