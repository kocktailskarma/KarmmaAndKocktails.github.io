Our Story

The Story of Karmma & Kocktails

Karmma & Kocktails brings Nepali and Indian favorites to Grapevine and Dallas with a menu built around bold flavors, comforting meals, and fresh-made favorites.

From momos and thali plates to curries, biryanis, noodles, and tea drinks, our kitchen focuses on generous portions, familiar comfort, and dishes guests will want to come back for.
