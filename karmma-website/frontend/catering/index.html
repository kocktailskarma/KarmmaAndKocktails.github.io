What We Cater

We cater office lunches, family parties, birthdays, celebrations and community events in Grapevine, Dallas and across the Dallas-Fort Worth area. Ask about momos, curries, biryani, tandoori and more from our menu.

How It Works

  1. Look at the Grapevine menu or the Dallas menu to see what your group would like.
  2. Call the location nearest you.
  3. Confirm your order and the pickup or service details on the phone.
