Our Story

Karmma & Kocktails began as a heartfelt dream to bring the vibrant flavors and rich traditions of Nepal to the heart of Texas. What started as a small family venture has blossomed into a beloved dining destination where heritage meets hospitality.

Inspired by the timeless beauty of Swayambhunath, the serenity of Boudhanath Stupa, and the warmth of Newari culture, our restaurant celebrates the soulful connection between food, culture, and community.

Our talented chefs and mixologists blend the spirit of Nepal with Texas flair — from authentic Newari delicacies to innovative Nepali-inspired cocktails — each dish and drink is a tribute to our roots and our journey.

Over the years, Karmma & Kocktails has become more than just a restaurant; it’s a gathering place where stories are shared, cultures intertwine, and every guest feels a sense of home.
